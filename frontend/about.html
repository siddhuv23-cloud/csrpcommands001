Who We Are

Your Definitive Guide to Grand RP Commands

We created CS RP Commands Zone to help every player — from rookie to veteran — master the world of Grand RP. Too many players miss out on the full experience simply because they don't know the commands and rules that govern each organization.

Our mission is simple: document every role-playing command, organize them by faction, and make them easy to find. Whether you're in the Government, FIB, LSPD, SAHP, EMS, National Guard, or Lifeinvader — we've got you covered.

We are continuously expanding our coverage, adding new commands, rule updates, and organization guides as the server evolves. This is a living resource, made with passion by active community members.
