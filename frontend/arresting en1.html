Process
Press Flex Z with no weapon equipped to handcuff the suspect. Warn them if they resist — use force only if necessary.
SAY
I am going to be putting you in handcuffs now, please stand still.
Press Flex X to grab the suspect by the arm and escort them to the vehicle. Remind them to watch their head.
SAY
I am going to be grabbing you by the arm now.
Press Flex G on the suspect to place them inside the vehicle. Inform them of all charges at this point.
SAY
I am going to be placing you in the vehicle now, please watch your head.
Drive carefully to DOC — do not exceed the speed limit during transport.
Dispatch Call
DISP
(BADGE NUMBER) to dispatch, I am 10-17 to DOC with (NUMBER) of 10-15 and (ADDITIONALS/CONVOY).
Miranda Rights
If the suspect doesn't respond, wait 5 seconds and repeat. Do this a total of 3 times. After the third ignore, inform them their rights have been read.
SAY
You are under arrest,
SAY
You have the right to remain silent,
SAY
Anything you say can and will be used against you in the court of law.
SAY
You have the right to an attorney,
SAY
If you cannot afford an attorney, one will be appointed to you by the state if available.
SAY
Do you understand the rights I just read to you?
Pause the 25-minute timer if the suspect requests a lawyer.
DISP
(BADGE NUMBER) to dispatch, my 10-15 requests a state lawyer at DOC.
Private Lawyer
Get their phone number immediately. Ask to see their lawyer's license. If unavailable after 15 minutes or 3 attempts, offer a state lawyer instead.
State Lawyer
Contact dispatch to check available options.
Approach the vehicle and press Flex G on the car. Select Passengers > Click to extract the suspect.
SAY
Taking you out of the car, please watch your head.
SAY
I am going to be grabbing you by the arm now.
/me
takes the XYZ size jumpsuit from the desk
Replace XYZ with the exact size the 10-15 requested. Take the suspect to a cell and lock the door behind you. Tell them you will check their pockets for their passport.
Always escort the suspect to a cell and lock the doors. Prepare the jumpsuit and inform the suspect you will check their pockets for a passport.
/me
drops the jumpsuit on the bed
/me
reaches into suspect's pockets to check passport
Searching the Suspect
Press Flex G, check organization info, and collect personal information. Search for illegal items — only AMMO-tagged firearms are allowed if licensed. Take a photo and log all confiscated items. Allow the suspect to remove their mask; ensure safety if they resist.
Charges & Sentencing
Inform the suspect of their accusations, sentence, and fines. Pull your PDA and enter all charges. Ask final questions and prepare to put them in the jumpsuit.
/me
puts suspect in jumpsuit
Press Flex G, select organization, and arrest the suspect. If sending to Isolation, adjust flex and arrest accordingly. Exit the cell and lock all doors.
Final Dispatch Confirmation
DISP
(BADGE NUMBER) to dispatch, last 10-15 is now 10-99, returning 10-19.
